01 · on your birthday

Salgirah mubarak ho aapko...

Aaj ka din mere liye sirf apki zindagi ka ek aur saal mukammal hone ka din nahi, balkeh us din ki yaad hai jis din is duniya ko aap jaisa insaan mila. Aur shayad apko is baat ka andaza bhi na ho ke apki zindagi ka yeh din mere liye kitni ahmiyat rakhta hai. Kuch tareekhein sirf calendar ka hissa nahi rehtin, woh dil ke qareeb ho jaati hain, aur apki salgirah mere liye unhi tareekhon mein se ek hai.

this date is one of those dates. ♡
02 · about you

Asal khoobsurati kirdaar mein hoti hai.

Main sochti hoon ke insaan ki khoobsurati aakhir hoti kya hai? Kya uske alfaaz, uska lehja, ya phir uska doosron ke sath bartao? Aur phir mujhe ehsaas hota hai ke asal khoobsurati in sab se kahin barh kar hoti hai. Woh insaan ke kirdaar mein hoti hai, uski niyyat mein, uske ikhlaaq mein aur us tareeqe mein jis se woh doosron ki zindagi mein apni jagah banata hai. Aur Azlaan, apki shakhsiyat mein aisi hi khoobiyan hain jo apko sirf ek achha insaan nahi, balkeh ek qabil-e-qadr dost banati hain.

03 · friendship

A friendship I value.

Apki sab se achhi baat shayad yeh hai ke aap meri zindagi mein un logon mein se ho jinhein main apna dost kehte hue dil se khushi mehsoos karti hoon. Dosti sirf roz baat karne, hansne ya ek doosre ke saath waqt guzarne ka naam nahi hoti. Dosti toh kisi ki maujoodgi ko qeemti samajhne, uski izzat karne aur uske hone ki qadr karne ka naam hai. Aur main chahti hoon ke apko yeh pata ho ke main apki dosti ko mamooli nahi samajhti. Aap mere liye un logon mein se ho jinki qadr unke door hone par nahi, balkeh unke paas hote hue bhi ki jaani chahiye.

Apki shakhsiyat ki tareef karne baithoon toh shayad alfaaz kam par jaayen, magar ek baat zaroor kahungi... apni achhaiyon ko kabhi mamooli mat samajhna. Duniya mein bohat se log milte hain, magar har shakhs apne peeche ek achha ehsaas nahi chhor jaata. Kuch log apni baaton se yaad rehte hain, kuch apne kirdaar se, aur kuch is wajah se ke unke saath reh kar insaan ko dosti ki khoobsurati par yaqeen aa jaata hai. Mere liye apki dosti ki ahmiyat isi aakhri baat mein hai.

04 · a dua for you

For your next 365.

Aaj apki salgirah par meri khwahish hai ke aap sirf khush na raho, balkeh apko zindagi ki woh saari khushiyan naseeb hon jin ke aap haqdar ho. Allah apke dil ko sukoon, apki rooh ko itminaan aur apki zindagi ko barkaton se bhar de. Apke liye jo raaste mushkil hon, Allah unhein aasaan farma de. Apki jaayez khwahishon ko qubooliyat ata kare, apki mehnat mein barkat de, apko har buri nazar aur har nuqsaan se mehfooz rakhe, aur apko aise logon ka saath ata kare jo apki qadr karein, apki izzat karein aur apke liye khair ka sabab banein.

Allah apko itni khushiyan ata kare ke apka dil shukr se bhar jaaye, itni himmat de ke aap mushkil waqt mein bhi umeed na haaro, aur itna sukoon de ke apko apni zindagi ki khoobsurati mehsoos hoti rahe. Aameen.

05 · remember this

You don't have to be your best self all the time.

Aur ek baat jo main chahti hoon ke aap hamesha yaad rakho.... Apko har waqt behtareen hone ki zaroorat nahi hai. Aapko har kisi ko khush karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Aap bhi insaan ho, aapke bhi bure din aa sakte hain, aap bhi thak sakte ho, aap bhi kabhi khud ko samajhne ke liye waqt le sakte ho. Magar in sab ke bawajood aap qeemat kam nahi ho jaati. Apni zaat ki qadr karna, apne sukoon ka khayal rakhna aur apni achhai ko duniya ki sakhti ke hawale na kar dena.

read this again on a bad day.
06 · wherever life takes us

Aane wale saalon mein...

Main nahi jaanti ke aane wale saalon mein zindagi hum dono ko kin raaston par le jaayegi. Waqt badlega, halaat badlenge, hamari zindagiyon mein naye log aur naye marahil aayenge. Magar meri yeh dua rahegi ke aap jahan bhi raho, jis bhi maqam par pahuncho, apke hisse mein izzat, sukoon aur kamyaabi aaye. Aur jab kabhi aap apni zindagi ke guzre hue dinon ko yaad karo, toh apko yeh ehsaas zaroor ho ke apki dosti kisi ke liye waqai qeemti thi.

Aakhir mein, main bas itna kehna chahti hoon ke apka meri zindagi mein dost ki haisiyat se hona mere liye khushi ki baat hai. Shayad main har baar alfaaz mein yeh sab bayaan na kar sakoon, magar iska matlab yeh nahi ke main apki qadr nahi karti. Kuch rishte roz izhaar ke mohtaaj nahi hote... unki ahmiyat insaan ke dil mein khamoshi se apni jagah bana leti hai.

07 · one last thing

Salgirah mubarak ho... Azlaan.

Allah kare apki zindagi ka aane wala saal apke liye guzre hue saalon se zyada khoobsurat ho. Apki aankhon mein khwab baaqi rahen, apke dil mein umeed baaqi rahe, apki muskurahat ki wajahen barhti rahen, aur apko zindagi baar baar yeh ehsaas dilaye ke apka hona bemaani nahi hai.

Aur haan, aaj ka din sirf apke liye mubarak nahi, mere liye bhi azeez hai, kyun ke aaj us insaan ka din hai jise main apna dost kehti hoon. Aur achhi dosti milna bhi zindagi ki un nematon mein se hai jin par jitna shukr ada kiya jaaye, kam hai.

Khush raho, abaad raho, aur hamesha apni khoobiyon ke saath yunhi qaim raho.
